What we collect

To run a speed test, your browser exchanges test data with our server. The server sees your IP address and the approximate location and network provider associated with it, which we display back to you. We do not store your test results on our servers.

Your recent results are saved only in your own browser (localStorage). You can delete them at any time with "Clear history".
