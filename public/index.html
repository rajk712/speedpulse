    What do these numbers mean?

    Download is how fast data reaches you: streaming, browsing, downloads. 25 Mbps handles one 4K stream; 100+ Mbps suits a busy household.

    Upload is how fast you send data: video calls, cloud backups, live streaming. 10 Mbps or more keeps HD calls smooth.

    Ping is the round-trip delay to the test server. Under 30 ms feels instant; gamers want under 20 ms.

    Jitter is how much ping varies. Under 10 ms keeps calls and games stable.

    For the most accurate result, use a wired connection and pause other downloads.
